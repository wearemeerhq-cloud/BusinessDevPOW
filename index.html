How I work

Business development in Web3 isn't just closing deals. It's finding out why a project isn't growing, then fixing that specific thing — whether it's a missing partnership, a broken signup flow, a team without a plan, or a community that doesn't trust the product yet. The projects below show four different problems and how I solved each one.

Finding and closing partnerships Growing communities Fixing team delivery Building user trust

The Projects

01

Syncore DAO

Ongoing
Product & Program Manager · Feb 2023 – Present
The problem

Four different teams — design, engineering, marketing, and operations — were all working without one clear plan. No one owned the task list, so deadlines kept slipping and nobody knew until it was too late.

What I did
  • Set up a simple, shared process so every team worked from the same plan and priorities
  • Took ownership of the task list, turning business goals into clear, actionable tasks
  • Checked progress every week so problems were caught early, not after deadlines were missed
  • Stepped in personally to clear roadblocks between teams
The result
Delivery scheduleNow predictable
Teams coordinated4
Roadblocks solved inDays, not weeks
02

Profunda Academy

Ongoing
Growth Marketing Manager, Web3 & Crypto · Jan 2024 – Present
The problem

The project had no partners bringing in new users. Almost everyone came from paid ads, and most of them left before ever becoming a paying, active user.

What I did
  • Found and closed partnership and internship deals with other Web3 communities
  • Built and led a small team to run campaigns on Discord, X, and Telegram
  • Worked with the product team to fix the signup process so fewer people dropped off
  • Tracked what was working so we could spend more time and money on it
The result
Program revenue+40%
Brand visibility+100%
Signups lost to drop-off−90%
Community engagement+60%

PW3 Academy / Axia Africa

Completed
Social Media Strategist & Community Manager · Feb 2023 – Jun 2024

  • Set up a monthly report so stakeholders could see real progress, not just likes and follows

05

Lunexa Protocol

Completed
Business Development Lead (Contract) · DeFi Lending Protocol · Mar 2022 – Nov 2022
The problem

Most of the money in the protocol came from a handful of large wallets. If even one of them pulled out, the whole protocol could become unstable — and new, smaller depositors didn't trust it enough to join.

What I did
  • Negotiated integration deals with 3 wallet apps to make depositing simple for everyday users, not just large holders
  • Partnered with an analytics provider to publish a public trust dashboard showing where funds were held
  • Ran a 6-week campaign with the new wallet partners to bring in smaller, first-time depositors
The result
New depositors+2,400
Funds held by top 5 wallets−35%
Wallet partnerships signed3
06

Nomad Chain

Completed
Ecosystem Partnerships Lead (Contract) · Layer-2 Blockchain · Aug 2022 – Feb 2023
The problem

The chain was fast and cheap to build on, but almost no outside developers were building apps on it. Without apps, there was nothing for users to actually do — growth had stalled.

What I did
  • Designed and launched a builder grants program to pay developers to bring their apps over
  • Closed partnerships with 2 developer-tooling companies to offer free access to teams building on the chain
  • Co-ran a hackathon with those partners to get new teams shipping in weeks, not months
The result
Live apps on the chain6 → 22
Developer signups+310%
Tooling partnerships signed2
07

Kaira Marketplace

Ongoing
Growth & Partnerships Consultant · NFT Marketplace for African Creators · May 2024 – Present
The problem

Creators were signing up and listing work, but buyers weren't completing purchases. Paying in crypto was an extra, confusing step, and buyers had no easy way to tell which creators were legitimate.

What I did
  • Negotiated a deal with a local fintech to let buyers pay with a card or bank transfer instead of crypto only
  • Built a verified-creator badge program so buyers could tell who was trustworthy at a glance
  • Created simple guides explaining how escrow protected buyers, to replace fear with a clear process
The result
Completed transactions+65%
Time to a creator's first sale−40%
Verified creators onboarded180+
